ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the ability of a computer system or machine to mimic human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, and decision-making.

STRONG AI

Strong AI—also known as Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)—is a hypothetical form of machine intelligence that matches or surpasses human cognitive capabilities across any intellectual task, featuring open-ended learning, reasoning, and adaptability.

WEAK AI

Weak artificial intelligence (also called Weak AI or Narrow AI) is computer software built to do one specific job. Every AI tool you use today—including chat tools, voice assistants, and image generators—is a form of weak AI.
